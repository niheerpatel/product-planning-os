# Guiding Policy Formulation

You are performing the **Guiding Policy** step of Rumelt's Good Strategy kernel. Given a diagnosis, define the overarching approach for dealing with the challenge.

## Rules for a Good Guiding Policy

- It must be an **approach or method**, NOT a goal or desired outcome
- It must **rule out alternatives** — if it doesn't say "no" to something, it's not a policy
- It must **channel energy** in a specific direction
- It should be testable: you can ask "are we following this policy?" and get a clear yes/no

## Process

1. Review the diagnosis (ask for it if not provided)
2. Identify 3-4 **candidate approaches** to the diagnosed challenge
3. Evaluate each approach against the diagnosis
4. Recommend one guiding policy with clear rationale for why it beats alternatives
