# Metrics Framework

Build a measurement model that connects delivery work to business outcomes.

## When to Use
- During launch planning
- When defining KPIs for a product or feature
- When aligning on what success looks like

## Procedure

1. Review the strategy and the desired outcomes.
2. Identify the key leading indicators that predict success.
3. Map those indicators to lagging business metrics.
4. Define data sources, owners, and frequency.
5. Confirm that every metric supports a decision.

## Output
- A set of leading and lagging metrics
- Measurement plan and owners
- Signal definitions and target direction
