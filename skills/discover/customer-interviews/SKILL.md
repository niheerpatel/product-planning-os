# Customer Interviews

Use customer interviews to uncover real user behavior, pain points, and decision drivers.

## When to Use
- Validating customer problems
- Exploring job-to-be-done and current workflow pain
- Testing assumptions before solution design

## Procedure

1. Define the target customer segment and research objective.
2. Build a script with open-ended questions.
3. Prioritize understanding behavior over opinions.
4. Capture quotes, evidence, and confidence levels.
5. Update product discovery artifacts with validated insights.

## Output
- Interview guide with sections for context, discovery, and wrap-up
- Key quotes mapped to problems and evidence strength
- Suggested next tests or follow-up interviews
